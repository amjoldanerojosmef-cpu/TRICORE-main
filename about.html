We are Tricore Digital Works, a team of three individuals united by our passion for technology and our ambition to turn our knowledge and skills into practical solutions for our customers.

01 / OUR STORY

Built on Passion.
Driven by Technology.

Tricore Digital Works began with three individuals — Keith, Shawn, and Aaron — sharing an idea: to build a business inspired by the course we graduated from and the technology we are passionate about.

What started as a plan among three friends became an opportunity to turn our knowledge, creativity, and shared interests into something meaningful. We believe that by combining our strengths and continuously learning, we can provide practical technology solutions that make a difference.

Our name represents the three minds behind the business, working together toward one goal: delivering solutions that our customers can trust.
